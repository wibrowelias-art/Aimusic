# STYLES.md - style research and the style families

Research date: 2026-11-12. This file is the source for the style profiles in
`src/style/profiles.ts`. It describes sound worlds in general terms. It holds no melodies,
no chord progressions, no lyrics and no note-level detail of any track, and the app never
sends an artist name or a song title anywhere.

## How this was made, and its limits

- Public text only: wikis, Bandcamp / label / artist pages, reviews, interviews, store and
  database listings. No audio was downloaded, streamed or analysed.
- Limit 1: the research environment could not open pages directly. Every fact below comes
  from web-search excerpts of the page behind the URL next to it. Quotes are "as excerpted";
  spot-check before reusing one in public.
- Limit 2: BPM / key numbers are third-party detector listings (Tunebat, SongBPM, Shazam,
  Beatport, Chordify). They are quoted as "listed at" and they often disagree.
- Nobody here has heard these tracks. Words about how something sounds are always somebody
  else's (named), never mine. "Not verified" means no source was found.
- Status of your notes: CONFIRMED / PARTLY / WRONG / NOT VERIFIED.

**Your note, checked.**

  harsh environment" and says of the track "even hell isn't all bad" (c418.org, repeated on
  the Minecraft Wiki); the wiki describes loud banging sounds at the start.
- "soft piano": mostly NOT VERIFIED. The Minecraft Wiki names synthesizer, organ chords, a
  distorted pad and clicking sounds, no piano. One fan wiki mentions a piano part later on.
  [fan wiki](https://c418wiki.absolutelywhizzer.co.uk/index.php?title=Minecraft_-_Volume_Beta)
- "warm synth pads": CONFIRMED in substance (Minecraft Wiki: "soft, warm, bubbly-sounding
  synthesizer music").

- "no vocals": CONFIRMED by absence (only instruments are ever listed).

**Profile.**

- Instruments: soft warm synthesizer, organ chords, a pad that fades in, clicking texture,
  metallic hits at the very start (Minecraft Wiki); a choir-like synth organ, later piano and

  frequent ([Epigram](https://epigram.org.uk/c418-the-music-of-minecraft-and-more/)).
- Vocals: none.

  No source describes the feel in words.

  [RBMA](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2015/08/c418-interview/),
  [c418.org gear post](https://c418.org/2017/01/26/what-does-daniel-use-to-make-music/)
- Mood (sources): "even hell isn't all bad"; the album is "both more positive and at times
  very dark" (composer, via Minecraft Wiki).
- Structure (general): through-composed; a percussive opening, a slow sustained body, some

**Not verified:** reverb length, amount of silence, whether piano is really there.

# The families

Five families, built from what the tracks share according to the sources. Each family is
what the app turns into a prompt: plain sound descriptors, no names. Where a descriptor comes
from your own note and not from a source, it says so - those are your taste, kept on purpose.

| Family (id) | Built from | Vocals by default |
|-------------|-----------|-------------------|
| Ambient keys + pads (`ambient-keys`) | 1, 3 | off |
| Bedroom demo (`bedroom-demo`) | 2, 7, 8 | off |
| Dream-folk vocal (`dream-folk`) | 6 | on |
| Slowcore guitar (`slowcore`) | 4, plus the band context of 8 | off |
| Slowed + reverb (`slowed-reverb`) | 9, 5 | off |

## A. Ambient keys + pads

- Shared traits (sources): ambient / quiet electronic; soft warm synthesizer, organ-like
  sustained chords, a pad that fades in, low synth bass, faint clicking texture; no vocals;
  through-composed with a fade-out; warm on top with something darker underneath.
- From your note (not in sources): piano as a main voice, lots of space, long reverb, calm.
  Piano stays in as "sparse soft piano notes" next to the synths.
- Tempo: no clear pulse; 60-84 BPM as a loose guide (84 is the listed value for track 1).
- Chain preset: big hall, light saturation, a little wobble, high-cut 10 kHz, hiss -56 dB.

## B. Bedroom demo

- Shared traits (sources): home recordings by one person; instrumental; closer to an ambient
  tone or drone than to a song; drum machines, synthesizers and guitar loops (collection
  level); four-track cassette / reel tape, tape slowed down, cheap instruments, cheap
  microphones; one idea, no sections.
- From your note (not in sources): loose, warm, unpolished.
- Vocals: off by default (the tracks are instrumental or most likely so). When switched on:
  a quiet, plain, close voice low in the mix.
- Tempo: 64-80 BPM, loose (listed values: 67 half-time, 74, 98).
- Chain preset: small room, strongest bedroom-mic setting, most saturation and wobble of the
  five, high-cut 9 kHz, hiss -50 dB.

## C. Dream-folk vocal

- Shared traits (sources): ambient folk / dream pop; slow repeating guitar lines, austere
  piano, minimal bass, synth drone, faint outdoor background sound; hushed, whispery female
  lead mixed low, with layered wordless humming behind it; self-recorded; dreamy, peaceful,
  meditative.
- From your note (not in sources): breathy, heavy reverb.
- Tempo: 60-80 BPM (listed: 63 and 95).
- Chain preset: big hall, light saturation, high-cut 11 kHz, hiss -56 dB, vocal blur 50 %
  (only acts when stems are separated).

## D. Slowcore guitar

- Shared traits (sources): slowcore / lo-fi indie rock / dream pop / shoegaze tags; electric
  and acoustic guitar, bass, simple live drums; home-recorded with the guitar plugged straight
  in; short instrumental interlude; "droopy haze", minimalist lo-fi arrangements.
- From your note: slow heavy guitars, mumbled vocals, loose drums - true of the band in
  general per reviews (softly spoken voice, gentle croon), not of the two reference tracks.
- Vocals: off by default. When switched on: softly spoken, gentle, low male-range voice,
  half-buried in the guitars.
- Tempo: 66-78 BPM (half-time reading of the listed 147).
- Chain preset: small room, medium bedroom mic, strong saturation, high-cut 10 kHz, hiss -52 dB.

## E. Slowed + reverb

- Shared traits (sources): ambient / dark ambient / downtempo electronica tags; instrumental;
  an official slowed version exists for both; the length ratio of track 9 points to about 86 %
  playback rate; the practice in general is "slow the whole thing down, then add reverb".
- From your note (not in sources): deep, blurry, reverb-heavy.
- Instruments are not documented for either track, so the prompt stays generic: soft
  sustained pads, a slow drone, distant bell-like tones, deep soft bass, little or no drums.
- Tempo: 84-100 BPM as generated; the chain then slows it by 14 % (to about 72-86).
- Chain preset: slowed 14 % (pitch down about 2.6 semitones) with the extra hall send, big
  hall on top, high-cut 8 kHz, hiss -54 dB.
